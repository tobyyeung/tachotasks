Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Tacho Tasks helps you organize tasks, projects, and your schedule. We use your information to provide those features. We do not sell your personal information or use your Google Calendar data for advertising.

1. Who operates Tacho Tasks

Tacho Tasks is operated by Toby Yeung. This policy covers the Tacho Tasks website and desktop application. For privacy questions or requests, email tobyyeungtwo@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google, we receive account identifiers and basic profile details such as your name, email address, and profile image. Google handles your Google password; Tacho Tasks does not receive it.
  • Workspace information: tasks, descriptions, projects, sections, dates, completion status, preferences, and other information you enter into the app.
  • Calendar information: if you grant access, we read calendar lists and event information, which can include titles, descriptions, times, locations, meeting links, organizers, and attendees where available. Calendar access is read-only; the integration does not create, edit, or delete your Google Calendar events.
  • Connection information: authentication sessions and access tokens needed to sign you in and connect to Google. When automatic calendar renewal is enabled and connected, encrypted credentials, including a refresh token, are stored by the backend to renew access.
  • Technical and support information: information you send when requesting help. Hosting, authentication, and download providers may also process IP addresses, browser or device information, request times, and operational logs when serving the app.

3. How we use information

We use information to authenticate you, save and synchronize your workspace across devices, display calendar events alongside tasks, remember preferences, provide support, and maintain the service. Calendar credentials are used only to support the calendar connection you authorize. Automatic renewal, where enabled, allows the app to obtain access without asking you to reconnect each time.

4. Google data and Limited Use

Tacho Tasks' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy and the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy, including their Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is not sold, used for targeted advertising, or used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models. We do not allow people to read Google user data except with your affirmative consent for specific information, when necessary for security or applicable law, or as otherwise permitted by Google's Limited Use requirements.

5. Storage and service providers

The web app stores workspace data, settings, authentication state, and cached calendar information in browser storage. The desktop app also uses local application storage. When cloud sync is used, workspace information is stored with Google Firebase; Firebase Authentication handles account sign-in. Calendar data can remain cached locally so it can be displayed again.

Google provides authentication, Calendar APIs, Firebase, and some web assets such as fonts. GitHub serves desktop downloads and updates. Hosting and infrastructure providers process information as needed to deliver their services, potentially in countries outside your own. Their handling of information is also described in their own privacy notices, including Google's Privacy Policy and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

We disclose information only as needed to operate requested features with service providers, with your consent, or where reasonably necessary to comply with law or protect the service and its users. We do not provide workspace or calendar data to advertising brokers.

6. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Workspace data is retained to provide your saved workspace until you delete it or request removal. Local copies and caches may remain on your devices until cleared. Signing out or uninstalling is not a request to delete your cloud account.

  • You can edit or delete workspace items using the app. Sync must complete for changes to reach other connected devices.
  • You can revoke Google access at Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not itself remove previously cached information.
  • Where automatic renewal is enabled, Stop automatic renewal removes the backend's stored calendar credentials. Cached events may remain.
  • To request access, a copy, correction, or deletion of your account and associated cloud data, email tobyyeungtwo@gmail.com from your account email. We may verify account ownership before acting. Please do not send passwords or tokens.

Some records may need to be retained for security, legal obligations, or resolving disputes. Provider backups and logs may persist under their retention processes. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location; contact us to exercise them or raise a concern. You may also have a right to complain to your local privacy regulator.

7. Security

We use measures such as authenticated cloud access and encrypted backend calendar credentials where automatic renewal is enabled. No system is completely secure. Protect your device and Google account, and be cautious when using shared devices.

8. Children

Tacho Tasks is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before using information in a materially different way.
